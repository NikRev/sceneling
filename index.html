English for everyday life

A little practice.
A more confident conversation.

SceneLing helps you practise English through short conversations in cafés, on trips, at work and at home. Lessons and progress live on your device.

Learn through situations

96 scenes in eight useful themes. Listen, choose a reply, put a sentence together and understand the meaning.

Build a regular habit

Continue an unfinished lesson, review phrases when they are due and use an optional daily reminder.

Choose what to practise

Grammar, listening, pronunciation, a searchable phrasebook and saved phrases. Difficult phrases come from your actual answers.

Keep your progress

No account is required. Export your progress file in Settings and import it on another device with SceneLing.

Common questions

Does the app need the internet?
Lessons and progress work offline. Audio uses the voices available on your iPhone. Support pages and sharing a progress file through an online service may need a connection.

Where are my voice recordings?
Pronunciation practice records a short temporary file on your device. A new recording replaces the previous recording. SceneLing does not upload recordings or score your pronunciation.

How do I move to a new phone?
Export a progress file from Settings, keep a copy somewhere you control, then import it on the new device. Import replaces current progress after your confirmation.

What does it cost?
The launch version has no paid lessons, advertising or in-app purchases.
