Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026 · SceneLing for iOS

SceneLing is developed by Nikita Revin. The app does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your learning progress, voice recordings or personal information to the developer. It has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

Learning progress and settings

Completed lessons, answers, saved phrases, review schedules and settings are stored locally in the app’s storage. The app does not run a server or offer account-based synchronisation. Your device’s backup services are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Microphone and speech

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record pronunciation practice. A recording lasts up to 20 seconds and is saved as a temporary audio file on your device. The next recording replaces it; the operating system may clear temporary files. Recordings are not uploaded. Example speech uses iOS speech synthesis and the voices available on the device. The app does not use speech recognition or automatic pronunciation scoring.

Reminders

Daily reminders are optional local notifications. They do not require a server. You can turn them off in SceneLing or in iOS notification settings.

Export, import and deletion

You can export a JSON file containing learning progress and saved phrase identifiers. You choose where to save or share it. A service you choose may process the file under its own policy. Importing replaces current progress after confirmation and keeps a local copy of the previous file. Reset clears active learning progress and keeps a local backup. Deleting the app removes its local storage, subject to your device and backup settings. Exported files outside the app must be deleted separately by you.

Support and this website

If you email support, the email service processes the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process normal technical request information under its privacy statement. The site has no advertising or third-party analytics scripts.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact nik007rev@gmail.com. If app data practices change, this policy will be updated to explain them.
